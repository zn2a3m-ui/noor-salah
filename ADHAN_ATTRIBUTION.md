# Adhan recording attribution

The Android build downloads the following recordings from their original public sources and converts them to mono MP3 at 128 kbps for Android playback. No recitation content is cut or otherwise edited; conversion is for format and compatibility only.

1. **Madinah – “33937 ejaz215 call-to-prayer-from-the-prophet-s-mo”**
   - Creator/uploader: ejaz215
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:33937_ejaz215_call-to-prayer-from-the-prophet-s-mo.ogg
   - License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
   - Changes: transcoded from Ogg Vorbis to mono MP3, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps.

2. **Fajr adhan at Malmö Mosque – 19 August 2012**
   - Source author: Islamic Center Malmö; the source identifies Besim Azemi in the recording description.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eid_al-Fitr_Fajr_azan_at_Malm%C3%B6_Mosque_-_19_August_2012.webm
   - License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
   - Changes: audio stream extracted and transcoded from WebM/Opus to mono MP3, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps; video is not included.

3. **ICR Friday Prayer 2nd Adhan – 17 May 2024**
   - Caller/uploader: Asim Javed
   - Source: https://archive.org/details/icr-friday-prayer-2nd-adhan-17th-may-2024-9th-dhul-qadah-1445-by-asim-javed
   - License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
   - Changes: transcoded to mono MP3, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps.

The two CC BY recordings are redistributed with attribution, source links, license links, and change notices as required by the license. The CC0 recording is attributed as a courtesy.
