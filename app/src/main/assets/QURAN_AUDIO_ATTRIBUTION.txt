QURAN AUDIO ATTRIBUTION

Streaming and download provider: Al Quran Cloud / Islamic Network CDN
Documentation: https://alquran.cloud/cdn
Terms and conditions: https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions
CDN: https://cdn.islamic.network

The app streams each ayah in sequence to provide continuous surah playback.
Users may optionally download the ayahs of a complete surah for offline
listening. Audio files are stored in the app's private external files
directory and are not bundled in the APK.

Reciters offered in the app:
- Maher Al-Muaiqly (ar.mahermuaiqly)
- Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais (ar.sudais)
- Ahmed ibn Ali al-Ajamy (ar.ajamy)

reciters or their estates for free redistribution at published bitrates.
Streaming, embedding and downloading are permitted for personal and
educational use under the provider's terms. Copyright remains with the
reciters or their rights-holders. The app does not claim ownership of the
