Jordan method:
Authority named by the provider: Ministry of Awqaf, Islamic Affairs and Holy Places.
Fajr angle: 18 degrees.
Isha angle: 18 degrees.
Provider verification status: the Jordan method is marked "fitted" by the provider, meaning it was matched against the authority's published timetable. Individual astronomical times may differ from mosque announcements by one or two minutes.
Method/source details: https://prayertime.si/methods/
Official Jordan timetable: https://www.awqaf.gov.jo/ar/Pages/PrayerTime

The provider is an independent service, not a government agency. Its Jordan method
is identified as fitted to the Ministry's published timetable; individual
calculated times may differ by one or two minutes. For supported Jordanian cities,
the app uses the provider's monthly city timetable. For other locations or selected
calculation methods, it uses the coordinate endpoint with the selected method ID.
The app keeps provider attribution visible and falls back to its clearly labeled local
estimate when online data is unavailable or the user disables the connected source.
